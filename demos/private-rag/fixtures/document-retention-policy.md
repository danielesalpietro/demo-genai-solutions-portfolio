# Document Retention Policy

**Organization**: Acme Corp  
**Version**: 2.1  
**Effective Date**: 2024-01-01  
**Owner**: Legal & Compliance Department

---

## Purpose

This policy establishes the minimum retention periods for corporate documents and data at Acme Corp. It ensures compliance with applicable laws, supports business operations, and governs the orderly disposal of records that are no longer required.

---

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties who create, receive, or maintain records on behalf of Acme Corp, regardless of the medium (paper, electronic, cloud storage).

---

## Financial Records Retention

Financial records are subject to strict regulatory requirements. Financial records must be retained for a minimum of **7 years** in accordance with regulatory requirements. This includes:

- General ledger and subsidiary ledgers
- Accounts payable and receivable records
- Bank statements and reconciliations
- Tax returns, supporting schedules, and correspondence with tax authorities
- Payroll records and related documentation
- Audit reports and management letters
- Contracts with a financial obligation outstanding or recently concluded

After the 7-year period, records may be destroyed in accordance with Section 6 (Disposal Procedures) of this policy, unless ongoing litigation or regulatory investigation requires extended retention.

---

## Employee Records Retention

Employee personnel files and related records must be retained for a minimum of **7 years after the employment relationship ends**. This includes:

- Employment contracts and offer letters
- Performance reviews and disciplinary records
- Benefits enrollment and changes
- Training and certification records
- Termination documentation

Medical and health-related records must be kept separately from personnel files and retained for **10 years** after the end of employment in compliance with applicable health and safety legislation.

---

## Email and Electronic Communications

Business-related email and electronic communications must be retained for a minimum of **3 years** from the date of creation. Communications that:

- Form part of a contractual agreement: retain as long as the contract plus 7 years
- Relate to a legal dispute or regulatory inquiry: retain until the matter is fully resolved plus 2 years
- Are purely personal or social in nature: not subject to retention requirements

Employees must not delete business-relevant emails from corporate systems within the retention period. The IT department applies automated archival policies via the corporate email gateway.

---

## Disposal Procedures

Records that have reached the end of their retention period must be disposed of securely:

- **Paper**: cross-cut shredding by a certified destruction vendor
- **Electronic media**: cryptographic erasure or physical destruction per NIST 800-88

A disposal log must be maintained for 3 years recording what was destroyed, when, and by whom.

---

## Exceptions and Legal Holds

When Acme Corp is involved in litigation, regulatory investigation, or anticipates such, a **legal hold** overrides all standard retention periods. The Legal department issues legal hold notices; recipients must preserve all relevant records until the hold is lifted in writing.
