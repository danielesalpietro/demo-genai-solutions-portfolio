## Responsibilities

Maintainers are expected to:

- Review and approve Pull Requests that touch critical paths (see `CODEOWNERS`).
- Triage Issues and ensure acceptance criteria are defined before work begins.
- Monitor CI status and act on security alerts within 5 business days.
- Participate in monthly demo health checks.
- Escalate security findings per `SECURITY.md`.

## Becoming a maintainer

Open an Issue of type `enhancement` describing your intended contribution area.  
Maintainership is granted by consensus of existing maintainers after a track record of reviewed contributions.
